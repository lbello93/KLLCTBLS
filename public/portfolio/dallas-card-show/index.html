Dallas Card Show

The sponsorship program for one of the country's largest card shows, built from zero to $300K+.

The challenge

In 2022 one of the largest sports card shows in the country had no sponsorship program: no packages, no partners, no revenue. Our founder built it from scratch.

What we built

  • Launched the program from scratch in 2022
  • Multi-year deals with PSA, SGC, Beckett, Goldin and more
  • Activation across 750+ tables and 85,000+ sq ft
  • Influencer network and talent like Michael Irvin
